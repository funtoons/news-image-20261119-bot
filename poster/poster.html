Thursday, 19 November 2026

Thirty verified stories from every region — what happened, and what was first reported, on this day.
1

GTA VI rolls out worldwide

After a 13-year wait, Grand Theft Auto VI unlocks at midnight local time, starting in New Zealand and following the clock around the globe. Early reviews give it 99 on Metacritic; pre-orders top $1.5bn.

2

Anthropic’s record IPO debuts

Anthropic prices its IPO at $83 a share, raising $81.3bn — the largest listing in history, past SpaceX. Shares begin trading on Nasdaq today under the ticker ANT, valuing the Claude maker near $1.5tn.

3

Trump, Xi and Putin meet at last

The first-ever trilateral summit of the three leaders takes place today in Shenzhen, on the sidelines of APEC. Nuclear arms and the Iran war are on the table; Ukraine, pointedly, is not.

4

Israel pushes into central Gaza

Israeli tanks cross the Yellow Line into Nuseirat and Bureij in a rare ground operation since the October 2025 ceasefire. Families flee as the army says it is clearing a tunnel; Hamas calls it a breach of the truce.

5

Crimea bridge hit a third time

Traffic is suspended over the 19 km Crimea bridge after a third attack in a week, Crimea’s Russian-installed governor says. Ukrainian drones and missiles had already destroyed its main road arch days earlier.

6

Jeffries nominated for Speaker

House Democrats nominate Hakeem Jeffries for Speaker by 251–11. If the full House elects him on 3 January, he becomes the first Black Speaker in the chamber’s 237-year history.

7

Cyclone Akvan bears down on India

Severe Cyclone Akvan churns towards India’s Andhra coast, forecast to turn very severe tonight with winds of 100–110 km/h. Landfall is expected on Friday between Machilipatnam and Puri; red rain alerts are out.

8

Voyager 1, one light-day away

NASA confirms today that Voyager 1 is now one light-day from Earth — about 16 billion miles, the first human-made object ever that far out. A radio signal sent now takes a full day to reach it.

9

Gulf war widens to Kuwait

After US jets struck a missile site on Iran’s Qeshm Island, Iran hits a US base in Kuwait — its first strike there since September — and attacks Bahrain again. Three US carrier groups are now in the region.

10

Pew: Islam largest by 2035

Pew projects Muslims will pass Christians as the world’s largest faith by 2035 — 2.57bn to 2.52bn — driven by higher fertility. Both faiths keep growing; by 2070, Muslims would number 3.6bn.
